T6iris - Iris Studio for Black Ops II (Redacted)
================================================

A movie-making editor for Black Ops II films (theater demos): free, dolly and bone
cameras, keyframes, depth of field with bokeh, lens flares, lens dirt, camera
lenses and shake, sky replacement, colour grading and recording to video.

Built on IWXMVM (https://github.com/reallyluckyy/IWXMVM) by reallyluckyy and its
contributors, through Iris Studio; Black Ops II runs with Plutonium's files
(https://plutonium.pw).

New in 1.3.4 (a test release):
- Recording works when the Black Ops II folder has spaces in its name (as
  "E:\games\bo2 t6"): before, ffmpeg couldn't start there and nothing was saved.

New in 1.3.3:
- Two or more lights now all show: the game lights one spot light at a time, so
  any spot after the first is drawn as a point light along its beam.
- The editor's top bar shows just the version.

New in 1.3.2:
- Green screen works in Black Ops II: the map is taken away so only what you keep
  stays over the green.
- Green screen ticks for each part: Players, Actors, Props, Guns (the guns they hold
  and the first person gun) and World (keep the map, green only where the sky is).

New in 1.3.1:
- A new look on a first start: black glass with white text, round corners, and the
  Alte Haas Grotesk font (Preferences > Style changes any of it).
- Getting started is now a short tour of each tab; click one to open it.
- The dolly camera starts where your free camera was when you switch to it (until
  the campath has nodes).

New in 1.3:
- Killstreaks as actors: RC-XD, A.G.R., Sentry Gun, Guardian, Care Package, dogs,
  and the air ones (UAV, Hunter Killer, Stealth Chopper, Escort Drone, Dragonfire,
  VTOL Warship, Lodestar, Warthog, Lightning Strike...), with moving parts
  (wheels, rotors, turrets), their lights, exhaust and blasts, and guns that fire.
- Shoots at: an actor can aim at another actor; it turns to face it, and its shots
  flash toward it and hit it.
- A new editor look: the tabs as icons on the right, the panel as a card, and
  Glass panels (Preferences > Style): a liquid gradient whose colours you pick,
  or your own wallpaper. Smoother tab, page and section animations, and more
  compact controls.
- In a film, the view keeps the game's shape, so the picture fills it.
- Multi-pass recording made simpler: named streams (Full frame, Green screen,
  World only, No gun, Gun only, Depth, Normals), and the green screen can keep
  players, actors or props.
- Run a game .cfg (Look tab), and go back to the defaults after.

New in 1.2:
- The multiplayer gun animations (pt_): firing, reloading, raising, putting away,
  grenade throws and flinches for every weapon, played on the upper body over the
  legs, with previews in the browser and on the actor.
- Effects on their own: Add > Add Effect... puts any of the map's effects (fire,
  smoke, explosions) where the camera looks, on its own bar on the timeline.
- Smoother editing of actors' events (a Hit's death no longer lags the panel), and
  blends between an actor's clips weighed properly.

New in 1.1: actors and props in your films.
- Add actors (any multiplayer character, body and head) and props (the map's models);
  move and turn them with the gizmo (click one; R switches move/rotate).
- Animations: every multiplayer full-body animation, and the campaign's (from
  T6iris-campaign-animations.bin), with a preview as you browse; paths to walk on.
- Guns in the hand and on the back, with their attachments and multiplayer camos.
- Events on the timeline: shots (muzzle flash, sound, casing), hits (blood) and
  effects on a bone; show and hide.


INSTALL
-------

1. Close the game.
2. Copy the "Plugins" folder from this zip into your Black Ops II folder (the one
   with t6mp.exe / the Redacted client), and let it merge with the Plugins folder
   that's already there.

   Also copy T6iris-campaign-animations.bin into your Black Ops II folder itself
   (next to t6mp.exe), for the campaign's animations.

   You should end up with:
       <Black Ops II>\Plugins\T6iris.Red32n
       <Black Ops II>\Plugins\T6iris\ffmpeg.exe
       <Black Ops II>\T6iris-campaign-animations.bin

3. Start the game with Redacted as usual.


USE
---

- Press F1 to show or hide the editor.
- Open the Demo tab and press PLAY on a film, or start one from the game's Theater.
- Recordings go to <Black Ops II>\iris\recordings unless you pick another folder in
  the Record tab. Your settings and keyframes are kept in <Black Ops II>\iris too.


IF IT DOESN'T SHOW UP
---------------------

- Check that T6iris.Red32n is directly inside the Plugins folder (not in a
  subfolder of it).
- Install the DirectX End-User Runtime (June 2010) from Microsoft:
  https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/download/details.aspx?id=8109
  Most Black Ops II installs already have it.


KNOWN LIMITS (TEST RELEASE)
---------------------------

- Actors' Look event (turning the head), shot tracers and bullet impacts don't
  play yet.
- A gun animation that walks (pt_reload_crouchwalk) previews over still legs.
- An effect on an actor plays where the bone is when it goes off; it doesn't
  follow the bone after.
- Gold, Diamond and the other special camos aren't there yet; a camo shows once
  the game has loaded its images.


REPORTING A PROBLEM
-------------------

Send the file <Black Ops II>\IWXMVM.log (and IWXMVM.1.log if there is one) with
what you did just before it went wrong. If the game froze or crashed, send it
before starting the game again: each start begins a new log.


UNINSTALL
---------

Delete Plugins\T6iris.Red32n, the Plugins\T6iris folder and
T6iris-campaign-animations.bin. The T6iris-*.txt files the game folder may have
are T6iris's settings for packs; they can go too.
