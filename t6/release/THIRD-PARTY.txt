Plugins\T6iris\ffmpeg.exe is FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), licensed under the GPL v3,
built by Gyan Doshi (https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/). Its source code is at
https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg and the build's at https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg.

T6iris-campaign-animations.bin holds animations of Black Ops II's campaign, read from
the game's zone/all/common.ff with T6iris's t6/tools/animations (in its source code),
which is built against OpenAssetTools (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/Laupetin/OpenAssetTools).
The animations are Activision's, from the game; the file only lets the multiplayer
game use what the campaign already has.
